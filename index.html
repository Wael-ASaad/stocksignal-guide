How StockSignal Works
StockSignal evaluates 5 dimensions per brand — Sales Trend, Aged Stock, Purchase Orders, Replenishment Need, and Efficiency (DIO/ITR) — and returns a single action label per brand using a SWITCH(TRUE()) priority chain. The first matching condition wins. 27 signals cover every inventory state from Critical (immediate action) to Healthy (maintain course).
